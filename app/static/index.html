UTF-8, comma-delimited, up to 2 MB / 10,000 rows. Four distinct columns are required. IDs may be blank when an email is present; emails may be blank when an ID is present. Blank expiration means no expiration. Duplicate IDs or emails reject the whole import.

Replacement is atomic. Invalid files leave existing members unchanged. Keep your original CSV as a backup.
